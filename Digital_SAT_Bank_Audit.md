# Digital SAT bank audit

Original items. Official Bluebook directions (Fall 2026) and practice tests 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, and 11 were used for structure only. No College Board question is reprinted.

## Blueprint used

- Reading and Writing: 54 questions, two modules of 27, 32 minutes each. All four-option, one best answer.
- Math: 44 questions, two modules of 22, 35 minutes each. Calculator allowed throughout. About 75% multiple choice and about 25% student-produced response.
- A module cannot be reopened. Figures are drawn to scale unless marked. SPR answers reject mixed numbers and symbols.

The practice banks scale those weights to 1,000 items each.

Numeric math answers were recomputed from the printed stem by a second solver, not by replaying the generator. That covers linear equations and inequalities, slopes, systems, percent, ratio, mean, median, probability, quadratics, radicals, rational equations, exponents, function values, circles, similarity, special right triangles, arc length, and the statistical-claim designs.

Reading and Writing keys were checked against the grammar or the data, not against the stored index: subject-verb agreement, who/whom, its/it's, parallel structure, past-tense coordination, neither/nor with the nearer subject, conjunctive adverbs, nonrestrictive appositives, transition family, rhetorical-synthesis goals, and every number cited from a table.
